Key Quantitative Findings
Core data science conclusions established from the empirical dataset
1. Extreme Market Concentration
Bitcoin holds 59.5% of market capitalization and the top 3 capture 77.8%. The average daily Herfindahl-Hirschman Index is 3,753, establishing an oligopoly structure.
2. 55x Volatility Disparity
Stablecoins average 0.06% median daily movement versus 3.33% for volatile tokens, proving algorithmic classification (CV < 1%) cleanly isolates risk profiles.
3. Weekend Volume Slump (-36.5%)
Market volume drops from $189.3B on weekdays to $120.2B on weekends. Welch's t-test ($t = -5.03, p = 5.9 \times 10^{-6}$) proves the contraction is statistically significant.
4. Fat-Tailed Returns (Kurtosis ~350)
Daily returns exhibit excess kurtosis of ~350 with 3-sigma outliers occurring 3.4x more often than Gaussian theory predicts, proving traditional VaR models underestimate crash risk.
